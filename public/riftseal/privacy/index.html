  • 랭킹 기록·결제 기록의 열람, 정정, 삭제, 처리 정지를 요청할 수 있습니다.

Rift Seal! Magical Girls — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026 · Operator: Kyeol · Contact: support@kyeol.win

Kyeol ("we") operates the mobile game "Rift Seal! Magical Girls" (the "Game"). The Game has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

1. Data we process and why

DataPurposeWhen
Game progress (stages, items, settings)Saving your game — stored only on your device, never sent to our serversWhile playing
Anonymous identifier (auto-generated per device), nickname, best stage, powerRunning and showing the leaderboard; blocking cheated recordsWhen you join the leaderboard (enter a nickname)
Purchase receipt data (order ID, product ID, purchase time)Delivering paid items, preventing duplicate or forged purchases, refundsWhen you buy an item
Advertising ID, device and app information, IP addressServing, measuring and protecting rewarded ads (processed by the ad provider)When you watch an ad

2. Retention

  • Leaderboard data: 1 year after your last record, or until you ask us to delete it
  • Purchase receipt data: 5 years, as required by Korean consumer protection law
  • Game data on your device: deleted when you uninstall or use Settings › Reset Progress in the Game

3. Service providers and international transfer

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

4. Your rights

  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your leaderboard and purchase records, or to stop processing.
  • Email support@kyeol.win with the user ID shown in the Game's Settings. We respond within 10 days.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • If you do not join the leaderboard, no data is sent to our server.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14 (under 13 in the US), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Parents or guardians can request deletion at support@kyeol.win.

6. Changes

We will announce changes on this page and in the Game at least 7 days before they take effect.
