균열봉인! 마법소녀 개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026년 10월 12일 · 운영: 결(Kyeol) · 문의: support@kyeol.win

결(이하 "회사")은 모바일 게임 「균열봉인! 마법소녀」(이하 "게임")를 이용하는 분의 개인정보를 「개인정보 보호법」 등 관련 법령에 따라 처리합니다. 게임은 별도 회원가입이 없고, 이름·이메일·전화번호 같은 연락처를 받지 않습니다. 게임을 처음 실행하면 기기별 익명 이용자 ID가 자동으로 만들어지며, 원하는 경우 Google Play 게임즈 계정을 연결해 진행을 클라우드에 보관할 수 있습니다.

Rift Seal! Magical Girls — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026 · Operator: Kyeol · Contact: support@kyeol.win

Kyeol ("we") operates the mobile game "Rift Seal! Magical Girls" (the "Game"). The Game has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. An anonymous user ID is created automatically the first time you open the Game, and you can optionally link a Google Play Games account to keep your progress in the cloud.

2. Retention

  • User ID, gameplay records, cloud progress and leaderboard data: deleted immediately when you delete your account; otherwise destroyed 1 year after your last visit
  • Raw gameplay event samples: 90 days
  • Purchase receipt data: 5 years, as required by Korean consumer protection law (kept even after account deletion)
  • Deletion record (user ID and deletion time only): 1 year, to handle repeat requests or disputes
  • Game data on your device: deleted when you uninstall, use Settings › Reset Progress, or Settings › Account › Delete account

4. Your rights and account deletion

  • Delete in the Game: Settings › Account › Delete account — your user ID, Play Games link, cloud progress, leaderboard records, mail and gameplay records are deleted immediately, and progress on the device is reset.
  • Request deletion after uninstalling: follow kyeol.win/riftseal/delete and email support@kyeol.win. We complete requests within 10 days.
  • You can also ask to access, correct or stop processing your data at the same address, with the user ID shown in the Game's Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You can turn notifications off in the Game (Settings › Notifications) or in your device settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14 (under 13 in the US), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Parents or guardians can request deletion at support@kyeol.win.

6. Changes

We will announce changes on this page and in the Game at least 7 days before they take effect.
